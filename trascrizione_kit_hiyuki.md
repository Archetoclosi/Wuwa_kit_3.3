When Glacio Chafe is stacked to its max, the target will be frozen, and all stacks of Glacio Chafe will be removed. Struggle to accelerate your recovery from the frozen state. Glacio Chafe stacks up to 10 times by default. The higher the stacks, the more DMG dealt, and the longer frozen duration.
